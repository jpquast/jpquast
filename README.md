### Hi there 👋👨🏻‍💻

* I am an Automation Scientist managing the Lab Automation Facility (LAF) at the Department of Biosystems Science and Engineering (D-BSSE) at ETH Zurich, based in Basel 🤖🧪
* I did my PhD in Structural Proteomics and Systems Biology in the Picotti Lab at ETH Zurich, where I studied protein-metal interactions using mass spectrometry ⛰️🥼🥽🔬
* I am interested in:
  * Automation, in the lab and at home 🤖🏠
  * Tinkering with electronics and 3D printing, building sensors and IoT devices around ESP microcontrollers 🔌📡🖨️
  * Growing plants (love Alocasias). My aim is a fully automated plant setup! 🪴🌿
  * Biology, especially unstudied organisms 🧬🦠🪲🌱
  * Mass spectrometry and proteomics 🧪⚙️
  * Open-source software and data analysis in R 💻📊
